Features

Everything a tailoring business needs — in one app.

From the first measurement to the final delivery, TailorBoard follows the real shape of a tailoring order.

01

Customer management

Store contact details, measurements, birthdays and photos for every customer — searchable in seconds.

02

Order pipeline

Track every order through seven real stages: Cutting → Try-On → Final Stitch → Ironing → Ready → Sent → Delivered.

03

Invoicing

Generate professional invoices with your business details and bank account, ready to send or download.

04

Measurements

Custom measurement profiles per customer, so a repeat order never means re-measuring from scratch.

05

Shopping lists

Build and track fabric shopping lists so nothing gets forgotten on a market run.

06

Tasks & reminders

Set reminders for deliveries, pickups and follow-ups, alongside auto-created birthday and delivery alerts.

07

Smart notifications

Get notified on order status changes, upcoming deliveries and customer birthdays before your customer asks.

08

Quick contact

Call, email or message a customer on WhatsApp with a single tap from their profile.

09

Photo support

Attach reference photos to a customer or an order — styles, fabric swatches, fittings.

10

Real-time dashboard

See active orders, upcoming deliveries and reminders at a glance, updated live via Firebase.
