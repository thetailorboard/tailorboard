Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE — set on publish] · Applies to the TailorBoard mobile app and this website.

1. Introduction

The Tailor Board ("TailorBoard", "we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through the TailorBoard app and website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information: your name, email address and phone number when you sign up.
  • Business data you enter: customer names, contact details, measurements, order details and photos you choose to add.
  • Usage data needed to operate the app, such as crash logs and basic device information.

We do not collect your location, and we do not use cookies on this website beyond what's needed for basic functionality.

3. How we use your information

We use your information only to provide and improve the TailorBoard service — for example, to sync your data across devices, send you reminders and notifications you've set up, and respond to support requests.

4. Where your data is stored

App data is stored securely using Firebase, Google's cloud platform, with encryption in transit and at rest.

5. Third parties

We do not sell or share your personal data or your customers' data with advertisers or other third parties. We use trusted infrastructure providers (such as Firebase and, for payments, Paystack) solely to operate the app.

6. Your rights

You can request to access, correct, export or delete your data at any time by contacting us at thetailorboard@gmail.com.

7. Children's privacy

TailorBoard is intended for business use by adults and is not directed at children.

8. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll update the date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to thetailorboard@gmail.com.
