About us

A digital workshop for African tailors.

TailorBoard started as a simple question: why are tailors — running real, growing businesses — still tracking orders on paper?

Our story

Built after watching a workshop lose an order slip.

Tailoring businesses in Nigeria run on skill, trust and word of mouth — but the admin behind them is still mostly manual: measurement books, paper receipts, and orders remembered rather than tracked. A missed delivery date or a lost measurement book doesn't just cost time, it costs a customer.

TailorBoard was built to fix exactly that gap — a place to keep every customer, every measurement, every order and every invoice, without needing a laptop or an accountant.

Our mission

Empower African tailors with modern tools.

We're starting in Nigeria, with tailors, seamstresses and fashion designers who run small and growing businesses. The goal is simple: make it easier to run a tailoring business well, so more of them can grow.
