FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can't find what you need? Contact us directly.

Installation & setup

Is TailorBoard free?
Yes — completely free to download and use. No hidden charges and no ads.
What Android version do I need?
Android 8.0 or higher.
How do I install the APK?
Download it from our Download page, open the file from your downloads, and tap Install.
Is it safe to download?
Yes. TailorBoard only requests permissions it actually uses — for the features you see in the app.

Data & privacy

Is my customer data safe?
Yes. Data is stored with Firebase's encryption and is never sold or shared with third parties. See our Privacy Policy.
Can I export my data?
Yes — you can request a data export at any time by contacting us.
Do you have a privacy policy?
Yes, you can read it in full on our Privacy Policy page.
Do you track my location?
No. We only store the information you enter into the app.

Functionality

Can I back up my data?
Yes — your data syncs automatically to the cloud via Firebase.
Does the app work offline?
Partially. Core features work offline, and changes sync once you're back online.
How often do you update the app?
We aim for regular updates with new features and fixes.
Can I use TailorBoard on desktop or web?
Currently Android only. A web version is on the roadmap.

Support

I found a bug. What do I do?
Email us at thetailorboard@gmail.com with details and, if possible, a screenshot.
Can I request a feature?
Yes — send your idea to thetailorboard@gmail.com, we read every message.
